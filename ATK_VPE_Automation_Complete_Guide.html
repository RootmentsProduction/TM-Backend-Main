1. Executive Overview & System Purpose

1.1 The Toastmasters VPE Challenge

In Toastmasters International, the Vice President Education (VPE) is responsible for planning club meeting schedules, assigning roles, tracking member education progress (Pathways projects), and ensuring high meeting quality. A standard 2-hour Toastmasters meeting requires coordinating 12 to 16 distinct role players:

  • Toastmaster of the Day (TMOD): Directs the meeting agenda, theme, and stage transitions.
  • General Evaluator (GE): Oversees all meeting evaluations and role-player feedback.
  • Table Topics Master (TTM): Facilitates the impromptu speaking session.
  • Prepared Speakers (Slots 1 to 3): Deliver 5-7 minute Pathways speeches.
  • Speech Evaluators (Evaluators 1 to 3): Deliver targeted 2-3 minute speech evaluations.
  • Timer: Enforces strict speech and report time boundaries with timing green/yellow/red signals.
  • Ah-Counter: Tracks crutch words, filler pauses, and vocal hesitations.
  • Grammarian: Introduces Word of the Day, Idiom of the Day, and delivers comprehensive language reports.

Manually coordinating this via WhatsApp groups or individual phone calls requires hundreds of repetitive messages each week: chasing vacant roles, collecting speech project details, gathering themes, formatting grammar words, assembling introduction bios, and distributing agendas.

1.2 The ATK VPE Automation Solution

The ATK VPE Automation Engine replaces this manual overhead with a unified, conversational WhatsApp bot and modern web application. It operates autonomously in the background to:

🤖 Autonomous Scheduling & Reminders

Automatically detects unfilled roles, sends interactive WhatsApp announcements, and lets members reserve roles in real time with quick-reply buttons and selection menus.

📝 Multi-Stage Structured Data Collection

Gathers complex multi-field inputs through natural conversational flows (e.g. Word & Idiom of the Day, Speech Pathways levels, Project titles, and TMOD themes) with fault-tolerant parsing.

🔄 Cascading Information Distribution

As soon as information is captured (such as the meeting theme), the engine immediately broadcasts it to dependent role players (GE, TTM, Speakers, Evaluators, VPPR) without VPE intervention.

🛡️ Long-Range Slot Protection

Maintains a 60-day rolling lookahead. Confirmed speakers are protected from accidental removal; speaker reschedule requests keep the existing slot safe until the new date is verified.

1.3 Historical Evolution: From Google Apps Script to Node.js / Express MERN Stack

The system originally operated as a monolithic Google Apps Script (GAS) deployment bound to a Google Spreadsheet. While functional, Google Apps Script imposed severe limitations: 6-minute execution limits, quota throttling on UrlFetchApp, slow cold starts (3-5 seconds per webhook), and fragile spreadsheet-based cell concurrency.

In this MERN Stack Edition, the entire codebase was ported to run on Node.js and Express with direct MongoDB Atlas persistence. To ensure zero disruption, a specialized GAS V8 Compatibility Engine was developed, enabling all 13,800+ lines of battle-tested domain logic to execute without modification while achieving over 1000x execution speedup (~2ms response times).

4. Deep-Dive Operational Workflows & State Machines

4.1 Workflow A: Role Vacancy Discovery & Member Self-Selection

When a meeting is scheduled, the engine checks for unfilled roles and broadcasts announcements to active club members. Members can inspect vacancies and claim roles with a single tap.

Step 1: Vacancy Detection Scheduled Tick (Daily / Periodic)

Tasks.js: processVacantRoleReminders_() inspects the upcoming meeting in VPE Weekly Planner. It checks columns for TMOD, GE, TTM, Timer, Ah-Counter, and Grammarian. If vacancies exist, it checks hasReminderBeenSent_() to enforce cooldown periods.

Step 2: Utility Template Dispatch Outbound WhatsApp

The engine transmits the approved WhatsApp Utility template atk_role_reminder_v2 or v3:
"ATK Meeting {{1}} is scheduled for {{2}} as planned. Please tap below to see available roles."
Features a Quick Reply button: [View roles].

Step 3: Interactive List Display Immediate Reply

When the member taps [View roles], Engine.js: handleRoleMenuRequest_() queries available roles and immediately sends a native WhatsApp Interactive List Message showing the exact unfilled roles with descriptions.

Step 4: Role Claim & Atomic Reservation Member Selection

The member selects a role (e.g. "Toastmaster of the Day"). Engine.js: reserveRole_() executes an atomic check:
1. If role is still vacant: writes member's name into the Planner cell, sets vacancy task to Completed, mirrors assignment to API Meetings, and sends confirmation with role guidelines.
2. If already claimed by another member: politely informs the member and redisplays remaining vacancies.

4.2 Workflow B: Grammarian 2-Stage Multi-Line Collection (Locked Spec)

The Grammarian flow collects the Word of the Day and Idiom of the Day. It is designed to be completely resilient to natural user input, typos, extra linebreaks, and missing fields.

Grammarian Parser Robustness Rules (Workflow.js)
  • Flexible Headers: Matches Word:, Word of the day:, Meaning:, Usage:, Example:, Example usage:.
  • Multi-line Support: Long definitions spanning multiple lines are captured without premature truncation.
  • Partial Preserves: If member provides Word and Meaning but forgets Usage, the engine saves Word + Meaning and requests only the missing Usage.
  • Final Review Safety: Data is only handed off to the TMOD after the member explicitly clicks [Confirm].

4.3 Workflow C: TMOD Theme Collection & Cascading Broadcast

1. Theme Request to TMOD T-5 Days

The engine prompts the assigned TMOD: "Hi {{Name}}, please reply with the Meeting Theme for ATK Meeting {{No}}."

2. Theme Ingestion & Planner Update On Reply

Workflow.js: handleThemeSubmission_() captures the theme, saves it to WA Submissions, and writes it directly to column 4 ("Theme") of the meeting's row in VPE Weekly Planner.

3. Cascading Downstream Broadcast Immediate (Autonomous)

Workflow.js: sendMeetingThemeToAssignedRoles_() runs immediately. It identifies all members assigned to General Evaluator, Table Topics Master, Speakers, Evaluators, and Grammarian, and sends each of them a personalized notification with the theme.

4. VPPR Publicity Handoff Immediate

Workflow.js: sendThemeAndTmodToVppr_() notifies the Vice President Public Relations with the confirmed theme, TMOD name, and meeting date so poster creation and social media promotions can begin without delay.

5.3 Flutter Frontend Integration Guidelines

Critical Security & Design Rules for Frontend Integration
  1. Permanent Boundary: Flutter Web must never communicate directly with Google Sheets or store Meta API tokens. All interactions occur through POST /api/v1.
  2. ID References Only: The UI must submit domain IDs (member_id, item_id), never spreadsheet row or column coordinates.
  3. Drag-and-Drop Order: The backend respects the array sequence submitted in items. Flutter reordering simply posts the reordered array.
  4. Custom Sessions: Educational sessions or special segments use type: "session" with a blank code. Standard Toastmasters roles retain their standard codes (TMOD, S1, etc.).

7. Meta WhatsApp Cloud API Integration & Webhook Security

7.1 Meta Webhook Verification Handshake

When Meta establishes a webhook connection, it issues an HTTP GET request to /webhook with challenge parameters. The server validates the token and echoes the challenge: 

// GET /webhook Verification Logic (src/server.js)
const mode = req.query['hub.mode'];
const token = req.query['hub.verify_token'];
const challenge = req.query['hub.challenge'];

if (mode === 'subscribe' && token === process.env.VERIFY_TOKEN) {
  return res.status(200).send(challenge);
}
return res.status(403).send('Webhook verification failed');

7.2 Inbound Webhook Processing & Deduplication

Meta transmits WhatsApp messages via HTTP POST payloads to POST /webhook. The server executes a strict multi-layer filter before processing:

  1. Payload Inspection: Extracts messages and statuses arrays from entry[0].changes[0].value.
  2. Deduplication Guard: WhatsApp delivers webhooks with at-least-once semantics, causing occasional duplicates. Engine.js: isDuplicateWebhookMessage_() tracks incoming message.id values in cache and drops duplicates instantly.
  3. Member Resolution: Normalizes sender phone number (e.g. 919846000000) and matches it against Member Master / Member collection.
  4. State Router: Routes message to active conversation handlers (Grammarian flow, TMOD theme flow, role menu, or guided interview).

7.4 Development Sandbox Recipient Isolation Guard

Live Message Protection in DEV Mode

To prevent accidental WhatsApp spam to club members during development and testing, Engine.js: sendWhatsAppPayload_() checks configValue_('WA_SEND_MODE'). When set to TEST, outbound messages are only delivered to phone numbers listed in WA_TEST_RECIPIENTS. All other messages are safely mocked and logged to WA Outbox without calling Meta servers.

8. Programme Planning & The 60-Day Web Review System

8.1 Long-Range Slot Planning Architecture

Standard meeting planners only look ahead 1 week. ATK requires a 60-day rolling lookahead (approx. 8 meetings) to allow members to plan Pathways projects and balance prepared speeches with special educational sessions.

8.2 Programme Slot State Machine

Slot State Display Badge Operational Rules & Invariants
OPEN Open Slot is vacant and bookable by any qualified member through speech targeting or VPE assignment.
SPEAKER_CONFIRMED Speaker confirmed Member has confirmed speech booking. Protected state: Cannot be overwritten without speaker agreement.
MOVE_REQUESTED Move requested VPE requested speaker to reschedule. Original slot remains fully protected until speaker confirms new date.
VPE_HOLD VPE hold Slot reserved by VPE for an upcoming educational session, workshop, or guest speaker. Holds never auto-expire.
SESSION_CONFIRMED Session confirmed Confirmed educational session with Presenter, Session Title, and Time Allotted (minutes).

8.3 The Responsive Programme Review Web Interface

The system serves a dedicated web interface at GET /programme-review?token=<TOKEN>:

  • Token-Based Security: Accessed via HMAC-SHA256 signed review tokens (programmeReviewToken_()). Does not require password login.
  • Zero-Config Client Bridge: src/server.js injects a browser fetch bridge for google.script.run, allowing ProgrammeReview.html to function identically in standalone web browsers and Google Apps Script iframe environments.
  • Actions Supported: Keep Open, Hold for Session, Request Speaker Move, Confirm Session (with Title, Presenter, Duration), and Release Slot.

9. Operational Timeline: 3-Week Meeting Lifecycle Walkthrough

This timeline illustrates how the automation engine autonomously coordinates a meeting across 21 days:

T-21 Days Long-Range Programme Review & Slot Booking Phase: Planning

• System evaluates speaking history and invites candidate members for speech slots.
• VPE reviews the 60-day horizon via /programme-review, placing holds or confirming special sessions.

T-14 Days Role Vacancy Announcement & Self-Selection Phase: Role Allocation

• processVacantRoleReminders_() identifies open roles.
• Broadcasts atk_role_reminder_v2 template to active members.
• Members click [View roles] and claim roles interactively via WhatsApp.

T-7 Days Speaker Details & TMOD Confirmation Phase: Content Assembly

• System prompts confirmed speakers for Pathway, Level, Project, Title, and Time.
• Prompts TMOD for meeting theme.
• TMOD replies with theme ➔ System cascades theme to all assigned role players & VPPR.

T-4 Days Grammarian Collection & Role Introductions Phase: Language & Bios

• Grammarian conversational flow initiates: captures Word of the Day, then Idiom of the Day.
• Grammarian confirms package ➔ System delivers grammar package to TMOD.
• Role players prompted for introductions (custom text or default bio fallback).

T-2 Days Evaluator Matching & TMOD Handoff Phase: Handover

• Speech details sent to designated Speech Evaluators.
• Complete meeting introduction package handed off to TMOD for stage transitions.
• System compiles draft agenda.

T-1 Day Final VPE Agenda Approval & Meeting Day Phase: Execution

• Final readiness score calculated (Status.js).
• VPE reviews and confirms final agenda.
• Automated reminders dispatched to role players with venue and arrival instructions.
